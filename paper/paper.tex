\begin{abstract}
I use a compact Transformer encoder to forecast the next yearly sunspot value from 24 years of history. The split is chronological and normalization is fitted on the training period only.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question}
Can self-attention model useful structure in a small historical sunspot series?

\section{Data and Method}
The series produces 285 windows. I train on the first 228 and hold out the final 57. Each scalar input is projected to 24 dimensions and combined with a learned positional embedding. The encoder uses four attention heads, feed-forward width 48, one layer, and dropout 0.1.

\section{Training}
The model trains for 24 epochs with Adam at learning rate 0.002 and mean-squared error loss.

\section{Results}
After fitting normalization on the training period only, the recorded hold-out RMSE is 33.2499 and MAE is 23.8058.

\section{Limitations}
The dataset is small for a Transformer and the repository does not yet include simple forecasting baselines. Model complexity should not be mistaken for forecasting quality.

\section{Reproduction}
Install \texttt{requirements.txt} and run \texttt{python src/run\_experiment.py}.
