\begin{abstract}
A compact Transformer is evaluated against persistence and Ridge autoregression for chronological next-year sunspot forecasting.
\end{abstract}
\section{Results}
\begin{tabular}{lrr}
\toprule
Model & RMSE & MAE \\
\midrule
Persistence & 33.0187 & 25.1982 \\
Ridge & 19.2463 & 14.1705 \\
Transformer & 31.8124 & 23.5226 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\section{Interpretation}
The Transformer improves slightly over persistence but is substantially worse than Ridge in this recorded experiment.
