# Ethics, Scientific Limits and Responsible Interpretation

This repository uses a public scientific time series and contains no personal data. Its main risks are methodological overclaiming, inappropriate reuse of licensed data and false confidence in a complex model.

## Complexity is not evidence

A Transformer is not treated as superior because it is newer or more complex. The bundle includes simple and strong tabular baselines and accepts a negative Transformer result as scientifically informative.

## Forecast scope

The experiment is not a physical model of the Sun and is not validated for operational space-weather decisions. Forecast errors may differ substantially around solar maxima, across eras and under future regime changes.

## Data revision and license

Recent SILSO values can be provisional. The primary study therefore excludes provisional rows and records the source hash and last definitive month. SILSO data are CC BY-NC 4.0; downstream users must preserve required attribution and respect the non-commercial condition.

## Statistical limits

Monthly forecast errors are serially dependent. Descriptive moving-block bootstrap intervals reduce, but do not eliminate, the risk of overstating uncertainty. No result should be generalized beyond the defined horizons, data source, context and evaluation period without new validation.
