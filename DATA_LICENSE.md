## WDC-SILSO data

The upstream Sunspot Number data are provided by WDC-SILSO, Royal Observatory of Belgium, under **CC BY-NC 4.0**. This repository does not redistribute the source CSV and does not relicense SILSO data under MIT.

Required source attribution should identify:

> WDC-SILSO, Royal Observatory of Belgium, Brussels — Sunspot Number Version 2.0, DOI 10.24414/qnza-ac80.

## Generated research artifacts

Metrics, tables and figures in `results/` and `paper/results.*` are generated from the SILSO study input. Their presence in this repository does not remove upstream attribution or non-commercial obligations that may apply to reuse of data-derived material.

For SILSO's current data policy, consult the official SILSO data and citation pages.
