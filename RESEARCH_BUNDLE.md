# Research Bundle Evidence Contract

## Identity
**Area:** AI Engineering  
**Study:** compact Transformer versus simple autoregressive baselines  
**Dataset:** WDC-SILSO monthly mean total sunspot number, Version 2.0

## Required evidence
A research result must record:
- official source URL;
- SHA-256 of downloaded data;
- first and last observation dates;
- number of usable monthly observations;
- window length;
- chronological train/validation/test sizes;
- scaler fit boundary;
- model hyperparameters and seed;
- RMSE and MAE for persistence, ridge and Transformer;
- Transformer best validation epoch.

## Non-claims
The bundle does not claim general Transformer superiority, physical causal modeling of solar activity, or operational space-weather forecasting readiness.
